# Code of Conduct

We want HyperFrames C++ to be welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Our expectations

Be respectful and constructive, focus on technical merits, assume good intent, and offer feedback without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats or the sharing of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove violating content and may restrict access when necessary.

Please report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub, rather than sharing private details publicly. Reports will be reviewed as reasonably possible.
